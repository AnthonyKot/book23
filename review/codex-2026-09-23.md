  it is not yet true for the exploratory Chapters 2 and 3. Recheck it at publication rather than

balance check. The source-claim rows and archive were already present when this pass resumed.

Left for the author: verify the final-book promise about every handler and test before publication;
that cannot be proved while Chapters 2 and 3 remain prose drafts.
